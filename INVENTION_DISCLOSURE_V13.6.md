# Invention Disclosure Draft — Seminole Water God-Mode v13.6

This is a technical invention-disclosure draft, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of patentability. A patent professional should conduct a prior-art search and determine filing strategy before public disclosure.

## Candidate inventive concept

A provenance-aware environmental decision engine that resolves an address to a regulated water system, constructs scope-limited evidence claims, stress-tests the geographic assignment through counterfactual coordinate perturbations, and reaches a final result through origin-deduplicated multi-agent negotiation with hard epistemic vetoes.

## Candidate claim families

### 1. Counterfactual geospatial identity certification

A method that:

1. geocodes a submitted address;
2. identifies an initial utility polygon and PWS;
3. generates a plurality of coordinate perturbations around the geocoded point;
4. repeats polygon and PWS resolution for each perturbation;
5. computes assignment stability and alternate-system outcomes; and
6. conditions report confidence or acceptance on the stability result.

### 2. Origin-deduplicated agent negotiation

A method that:

1. receives structured claims from specialized evidence agents;
2. attaches evidence scope, provenance, confidence, and originating-sample identifiers;
3. collapses publisher replicas to one origin vote;
4. applies reliability-weighted support and opposition;
5. permits hard epistemic vetoes for prohibited scope transitions; and
6. produces accepted, rejected, and undecided claims with an auditable negotiation transcript.

### 3. Dual uncertainty outputs

A method that calculates separate confidence values for:

- system-level water evidence; and
- household-level exposure evidence,

where household confidence is capped unless an address-linked sample exists.

### 4. Robustness-certified environmental reporting

A method that issues a machine-readable certificate only after testing provider identity, coordinate stability, evidence scope, negotiated consensus, contradiction load, and confidence thresholds.

### 5. Verdict-flip evidence acquisition

A method that ranks additional data requests using expected conclusion-change probability, expected confidence gain, health severity, and acquisition effort.

### 6. Censoring-aware peer-system comparison

A method that compares water systems only when analytes, units, dates, evidence scope, and censoring status are compatible, while excluding non-detect and missing values from zero-based ranking.

## Technical distinction

The candidate novelty is not the isolated use of point-in-polygon, Bayesian updating, argumentation, or environmental databases. The candidate inventive contribution is the constrained combination of geospatial counterfactual testing, provenance-aware origin deduplication, hard evidence-scope vetoes, auditable negotiation, dual uncertainty outputs, and robustness certification in an address-resolved environmental evidence engine.
