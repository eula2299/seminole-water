# Invention Disclosure Addendum — v13.7 Causal-Conformal Evidence Assurance

## Proposed technical title

**A Novel Causal-Conformal, Provenance-Deduplicated, Set-Valued Multi-Agent Environmental Evidence Negotiation Platform with Monotone Scope Applicability, Origin-Level Contradiction Cut Sets, Source-to-Tap Proof Graphs, and Budgeted Evidence Acquisition**

## Candidate invention family 1: Set-valued utility identity with counterfactual and conformal constraints

A computer-implemented method that:

1. generates multiple public-water-system candidates from geospatial and administrative evidence;
2. computes candidate nonconformity scores;
3. creates a set-valued provider output using held-out calibration residuals when available;
4. combines the set with coordinate-perturbation stability;
5. prevents a singleton provider assertion when ambiguity remains.

Potential technical effect: reduces false provider assignment while retaining useful bounded outputs.

## Candidate invention family 2: Monotone environmental evidence applicability lattice

A system that:

1. assigns evidence to an ordered geographic/causal scope;
2. scores spatial, temporal, hydraulic, provenance, laboratory, and regulatory compatibility;
3. permits evidence to support equal or coarser scopes;
4. blocks promotion to a finer scope through hard monotonicity constraints;
5. produces separate applicability fractions for system and household claims.

Potential technical effect: prevents systematic scope inflation in environmental evidence fusion.

## Candidate invention family 3: Origin-removal negotiation influence stress testing

A method that:

1. groups publisher replicas by originating evidence unit;
2. removes one origin group at a time;
3. reruns the complete multi-agent negotiation;
4. measures claim-status flips and confidence changes;
5. identifies single-origin fragility.

Potential technical effect: reveals hidden evidence concentration that ordinary source counting misses.

## Candidate invention family 4: Minimum origin-level contradiction cut-set localization

A method that:

1. constructs explicit support/opposition claim groups;
2. maps each claim to originating evidence units;
3. searches for minimum origin-removal sets resolving all contradictions;
4. reports multiple minimal cut sets where they exist;
5. preserves the distinction between contradiction localization and evidence rejection.

Potential technical effect: makes conflicting environmental conclusions auditable and computationally localizable.

## Candidate invention family 5: Negative-evidence coverage tensor

A system that:

1. creates a multidimensional monitoring tensor indexed by contaminant, time, source family, and evidence scope;
2. distinguishes detections, censored non-detects, measurements, stale cells, and missing cells;
3. prevents missing or censored cells from being represented as zero;
4. limits absence claims to monitored cells;
5. feeds coverage uncertainty into the final report.

Potential technical effect: reduces false reassurance caused by incomplete monitoring coverage.

## Candidate invention family 6: Causal source-to-tap proof graph

A system that:

1. represents source water, treatment, distribution, service area, premise, and tap as nodes;
2. labels edges as observed, inferred, or absent;
3. computes a bottleneck confidence and strongest supported evidence scope;
4. requires a fully observed pathway before household concentration causality is asserted;
5. integrates hydraulic reachability and address-linked samples.

Potential technical effect: converts implicit water-path assumptions into a machine-auditable causal proof structure.

## Candidate invention family 7: Multi-agent evidence acquisition auction

A method that:

1. receives evidence requests from multiple agents;
2. scores each request by expected confidence gain, verdict-flip probability, health severity, effort, latency, and privacy;
3. builds a Pareto frontier;
4. allocates a finite acquisition budget;
5. returns an ordered, constraint-compliant evidence plan.

Potential technical effect: improves decision accuracy under bounded time, cost, and privacy constraints.

## Candidate invention family 8: Adversarial environmental verdict envelope

A method that:

1. identifies structured fragility scenarios;
2. applies penalties or reruns under alternate-provider, origin-removal, coverage, causal-path, and negotiation-uncertainty conditions;
3. outputs pessimistic, base, and optimistic confidence;
4. labels stress stability without claiming a statistical confidence interval;
5. attaches the stress certificate to the evidence package.

Potential technical effect: exposes whether a conclusion survives plausible adverse evidence configurations.
