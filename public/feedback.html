What happens to your feedback?

Detailed feedback is sent privately to the IsMyWaterOK team and stored with the same private contact system used by the Contact Us page.

  • Your written feedback is never published publicly.
  • Your rating can contribute to aggregate “helpfulness” metrics.
  • If you allow a reply, your email is included so the team can follow up.

If your concern is an urgent water outage or boil-water notice, use your official utility instead of this feedback form.
