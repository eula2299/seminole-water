Disclaimers & Terms of Use

This is not a test of your home's water

This tool summarizes monitoring records for the public water system serving an area. Water can change inside a building because of plumbing, fixtures, filters, or service lines. Only a sample collected from your own tap and analyzed by a certified laboratory can tell you what is in the water at your faucet.

This information is not complete

The tool summarizes available public records and does not include everything that could be present in water. The absence of a result is not proof that a substance is absent.

This is not medical, legal, or professional advice

Nothing in this tool should be relied upon to make health, medical, legal, financial, or property decisions. For health concerns, consult an appropriate health professional or public-health agency.

Accuracy and currency of data

Results are drawn from public sources including U.S. EPA and Florida environmental records. Public datasets can contain errors, omissions, or delays, and monitoring results reflect conditions when samples were collected.

Independence

This tool is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by any utility or government agency.

For official information

Confirm important findings with the serving water utility, its Consumer Confidence Report, or the relevant government agency.
